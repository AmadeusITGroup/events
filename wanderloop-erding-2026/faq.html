Frequently asked questions

Practical information for attendees, sponsors, and partners at Wanderloop Erding 2026.

Attendees Sponsors & partners

For all attendees

When and where is the event?

Amadeus Wanderloop @ Erding 2026 takes place on 10–11 November 2026 at
Amadeus Data Processing GmbH, Berghamer Str. 6, 85435 Erding-Aufhausen, Germany.

How can I get to the venue from Munich?

By car

About 36 min · 36.8 km

  • Join the A94 eastbound toward Passau.
  • Continue via Flughafentangente (St 2580) toward Erding.
  • Exit toward Erding-Aufhausen and follow signs to Bergham.
Open driving directions

By public transport

About 1 hr 4 min from München Ost to Aufhausen, including the transfer

  • Take the S2 toward Erding from München Ost to Markt Schwaben (about 24 minutes).
  • Change to the S2 replacement bus toward Erding; the ride to Aufhausen takes about 30 minutes.
  • Get off at Aufhausen, then walk about 500 m (7 minutes) to the venue via Berghamer Str. or Pretzener Str.
  • Allow extra time: replacement buses are diverted between Ottenhofen and Erding.

Check the S-Bahn München operating situation for the latest replacement-bus timetable before travelling.

Open public-transport directions

From Munich Airport

About 20 min by taxi, depending on traffic

Which day should I register for?

Choose your event day based on eligibility:

  • 10 November is an afternoon of exchanges and networking dedicated to external tech leaders not working at Amadeus.
  • 11 November is a full day of tech talks and exchanges designed for Amadeus individual contributors, where external guests are also welcome.

Registration is separate for each day:

  • register for 10 November
  • and/or for 11 November

For sponsors & partners

When can external sponsors & vendors attend?

External sponsors and vendors are invited to attend on 11 November only.

What is provided at the booth?

Each booth includes a monitor, docking station, keyboard, and a mouse. A courtesy table (1 m × 1.8 m), chairs and electricity are also provided.

We encourage our partners to create an engaging booth for our tech-savvy audience.

When can I install and dismantle my booth?
  • Installation: 11 November from 15:30 – exception for heavy setups: 14:30.
  • Teardown: to be started after 17:45; and complete by 18:30.
Can I send parcels in advance?

Yes. You can send parcels in advance and arrange for them to be collected later. Contact the organizing team at devrel@amadeus.com to coordinate delivery and collection.

Who should I contact about partnering or sponsoring?

For partnership or sponsorship questions, contact the Amadeus DevRel team at devrel@amadeus.com.
